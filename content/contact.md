Questions, corrections, or just want to talk about random walks — email is the
most reliable way to reach me:

**[hello123@randomwalk.dev](mailto:hello123@randomwalk.dev)**

For anything about the site itself — a broken snippet, a rendering bug, a
reference that doesn't resolve — the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/feribg/randomwalk.dev/issues) is a better
home than email, since the fix ends up visible to whoever hits it next.
